This release is about the window itself, the list you share, and the adverts you
read. The rail replaces the row of six buttons, a shortlist can be chosen advert
by advert, duplicates from a country-level place or a missing employer are
caught, and an advert's paragraphs, headings and lists survive the trip from the
board.

### The window

- **A rail instead of a header full of buttons.** Scan and Check listings stay in
  the header, because they are what the window does; the places you can go moved
  to a column on the left: Matches, Pay, Applications, Shortlists, then Settings
  and Sources. Scan is the only filled button on screen. The selected row is
  clay, not blue: blue is the information colour here, and a highlighted
  destination is not information.
- **The profile is a menu, not a screen.** The selector under the wordmark lists
  your profiles with their posting counts and switches on click. The full "who's
  using jobscout?" screen remains on the Settings path, where profiles are
  managed.
- Sources opens Settings on the Job boards tab and shows how many boards are on
  before Settings has ever been opened.
- Counts sit on their rows (Matches 2413, Applications 12), tabular so they do
  not jitter as they change.

### Shortlists and the PDF

- **Named lists that outlive the window.** Lists are stored in the database, one
  advert can sit in several, and deleting the active list falls back to the
  oldest rather than losing the pointer.
- **A restart keeps your picks.** The grid used to be painted before the list
  was read, so every card showed a "+" again; the list is now loaded before the
  first render, and the card edge is drawn from the stored selection too.
- **Tick which adverts go into the PDF.** The screen has a checkbox per advert;
  no ticks means the whole list, and the button says which it will do ("Export 2
  selected"). A selection is exported in the list's own order, and ticks reset
  when you switch lists.
- **The PDF is designed, and its links survive a page.** Each advert is a card
  with a clay spine, labelled Salary / Contract / Deadline, and the app's mark
  beside the list name. The old file printed the raw board URL, which reportlab
  anchors per line: a wrapped URL was clickable only on its first line. The
  advert now carries a short underlined label ("stepstone.de · open advert") and
  the full URL lives in the link annotation, where wrapping cannot touch it.
- The grid's floating footer bar is gone; opening, naming and exporting happen on
  the Shortlists screen, and the result is reported there.

### Duplicates

- **A country is a place.** EURES writes "Switzerland" where jobs.ch writes
  "Zürich", so the two copies never matched. The cross-source pass accepts a
  country when one side names only a country, and still refuses Bremen against
  Munich.
- **A missing employer is not a different job.** LinkedIn stores many adverts
  with no company, which the strict pass skipped. A second pass matches them on
  the title with its trailing gloss removed, a compatible place, and at least 70%
  of the opening words shared. The copy that has an employer is the one that
  survives.
- A dead keeper no longer leaves its copies pointing at nothing, hidden for good
  while the board still had the advert. Purging promotes the oldest copy.
- Measured on the archaeology profile: 61 visible adverts became 58, with the
  Zürich role left as one EURES card plus the agency's own advert.

### Reading an advert

- **Paragraphs, headings and lists come back.** The generic extractor used
  `clean_html`, which joins every block into one line; the JSON-LD the boards
  publish carries the paragraphs and lists all along. A list item keeps its
  marker now, so it renders as a list and not as a line of prose.
- **Long text is no longer cut inside words.** The paragraph splitter accepted a
  full stop with no space after it, so "Gehalt ab 70.000 EUR" became "70." and
  "000 EUR", and "Node.js" became "Node." and "js/Express". A boundary now needs
  a space and a capital after it, a dot between digits is a thousands separator,
  and abbreviations (inkl., bzw., ca., z.B.) never end a sentence.
- Adverts already stored as one line cannot be un-flattened, only read again.
  The fill job re-reads long bodies with no line break once, at its usual pace
  per host, and only for adverts whose title matches the profile. Refresh in the
  advert's pane does it immediately for one advert.

### Check listings

- **A 200 that lands on another page is not a live advert.** karriere.at answers
  an expired advert with a redirect to its city page, so "Open ad" opened
  "Jobs in Brunn am Gebirge" and the check called the advert alive. The fetcher
  now reports where a request landed, and a redirect that drops the identifier in
  the path counts as gone. Measured on the 29 visible karriere.at adverts: 6 were
  redirecting to a city page and are now removed.

### Small things

- The shortlist + and tick are drawn, not glyphs, so they render the same in
  every webview.
- The scan summary no longer counts an advert twice when a teaser row and its
  detail were both added.
