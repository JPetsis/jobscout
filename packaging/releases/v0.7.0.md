This release adds two academic job boards, an optional language filter, and a
check that tells you which listings are still open. It also fixes two boards
that had been answering HTTP 200 while storing nothing.

### New sources

- **jobs.ac.uk** (United Kingdom). Server-rendered search, 25 results per page.
  Each card carries the department, the salary band and a closing date, and the
  closing date becomes the posting's expiry, so an advert that has closed is
  ruled out without a request.
- **THE unijobs** (Times Higher Education, United Kingdom and international).
  Carries European professorial adverts alongside British ones.

### Sources that had stopped working

- **Stepstone** returned no results for thirty consecutive scans while answering
  HTTP 200. Its title links are absolute now and the parser required a relative
  one, so every card was discarded. It stores listings again, with company, city
  and date.
- **Jobware** failed in the same silent way for eight scans after the site moved
  its detail link out of a data attribute into a plain anchor.
- **talent.com** no longer serves a page that can be parsed. Its results are
  delivered by Next.js Server Actions with a build-specific identifier and there
  is no public endpoint behind them, so the source is documented as unavailable
  and no longer spends scan time.
- SmartRecruiters and Workday advert pages now open from the stored row, not
  only from a fresh fetch.
- XING advert texts no longer end with the ten literal `null` tokens its own
  metadata contains. Twenty-three stored adverts were repaired.

### Searching

- Query generation learned that spelling variants of one word are one word.
  Previously `archäolog`, `archaeolog` and `archaolog` used all three query
  slots a board is given, so specialisms listed further down a profile, such as
  zooarchaeology or prehistory, were never searched for. They are now.
  `preferences.query_roles` sets how many distinct role words each board is
  asked for.
- **Language filter**, optional and per profile. `preferences.language_allow`
  keeps only adverts written in the listed languages. An advert with too little
  text to classify is kept rather than guessed at. Editable in Settings ▸ Where.

### Liveness and duplicates

- **Check listings** verifies the postings currently on the board. A board that
  publishes its whole list settles every row in one request; an incomplete list
  may confirm a row but never removes one. Rows a check finds gone are deleted,
  except anything you saved or applied to.
- **Early applicant** is shown as a pill only where the board states it, which is
  XING and LinkedIn; silence means unknown, not "no".
- **Duplicates**: one advert carried by two boards is stored once when the
  wording is identical, and the employer's own board is the copy kept.
  Translated adverts are deliberately not merged. Employer, city and grade also
  describe a whole hiring round, so a merge that hides a job is worse than a
  duplicate.

### Profiles and settings

- The app opens the profile you used last. The picker appears on first run, and
  remains available from Settings ▸ Profile.
- **Re-score postings** in the settings rail applies changed scoring settings to
  everything already stored, and marks itself when a scoring setting has changed
  but has not been applied.

### Interface

- The header is grouped by what each control does. Scan and Check listings act on
  the listing, Pay and Applications switch what the window shows, and the theme
  switcher and the Settings gear sit at the edge.
- The filter bar is split into a search row and a narrowing row, and every
  control in the chrome shares one height.
- Scan progress only moves forward. It counts finished boards rather than their
  position in the list, which parallel fetching had made meaningless.
- The contract chip is filled, so a permanent contract reads at a glance, and
  the palette is warm clay rather than green.
